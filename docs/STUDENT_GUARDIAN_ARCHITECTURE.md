# Swanford Academy — Student, Guardian & Relationship Architecture (Stage 6)

## 1. Executive Overview & Design Philosophy

Stage 6 establishes the authoritative people, student, guardian, and relationship foundation for the Swanford Academy Management System. This module operates as the single source of truth for:
- Student identity and lifecycle management.
- Parent/Guardian profiles and their one-to-one relationship with portal authentication user accounts.
- Flexible, historical Guardian-Student relationships with transactional primary contact invariants.
- Concurrent multi-programme academic enrollments (e.g. Nursery/Primary combined with Tahfeez).
- Field-level sensitive data visibility (medical notes, genotype, blood group, allergies, emergency contacts).
- Non-destructive duplicate warning detection and conservative guardian email matching.

All later modules (Stage 7 Admissions Workflow, Stage 8 Finance, Attendance, Assessments, and Portals) consume this layer without redesigning or duplicating people models.

## 2. Student Profile Architecture & Admission Numbers

### 2.1 Immutability of Admission Numbers
- **Server-Generated Only**: Admission numbers are strictly generated by the server via `reserveAdmissionNumberBlock` (`SA-YYYY-NNNN`).
- **Never Client-Supplied**: Client requests cannot pass or alter `admissionNumber`.
- **Permanent Identifier**: Once assigned upon enrollment or creation, `admissionNumber` cannot be changed via `updateStudent`. Any attempt to modify it is blocked at the service and transaction level.
- **Name & Demographic Updates**: Modifying a student's `firstName`, `lastName`, `dateOfBirth`, or `gender` writes an immutable audit record and preserves the original `admissionNumber`.

### 2.2 Student Status Decoupling
A student's global institutional lifecycle status (`Student.currentStatus`) is completely decoupled from their term-by-term programme enrollment status (`StudentProgrammeEnrollment.enrollmentStatus`).

| Student Status | Meaning & Invariants |
| :--- | :--- |
| `ACTIVE` | Currently in good standing with the institution; eligible for active enrollments. |
| `INACTIVE` | Temporarily absent, on extended medical/family leave; active enrollments paused. |
| `WITHDRAWN` | Formally withdrawn from Swanford Academy. All active programme enrollments terminated. |
| `GRADUATED` | Successfully completed final institutional level (e.g. Primary 6). Records archived. |
| `ARCHIVED` | Historical archive status. Protected by `STUDENT_ARCHIVE` permission. |

## 3. Guardian Architecture & Portal Account Linking

### 3.1 Person Entity vs Portal User Account
- A `Guardian` record represents the legal, biological, or custodial human entity in Dutse, Jigawa State.
- Guardians may not initially have access to the web portal; email address is optional (`String?`), recognizing practical northern Nigerian demographic realities where some parents communicate solely via SMS/phone.
- When an online portal account is created, a `User` record is provisioned with `RoleCode.PARENT` and linked to `Guardian.userId`.

### 3.2 1-to-1 User-to-Guardian Invariant
- **Strictly 1:1**: A `User` can only be linked to at most one `Guardian`, and a `Guardian` can only be linked to at most one `User` (`@unique` constraint on `Guardian.userId`).
- **Pre-Existing Link Rejection**: Attempting to link a `User` already bound to another guardian or linking a `Guardian` already bound to another user throws a transactional `AuthorizationError(400)`.

## 4. Guardian-Student Relationships & Primary Contact Invariants

### 4.1 Relationship Lifecycle & Restoration
The system supports:
- **One Active Relationship**: Exactly one active relationship record between a given guardian and a given student at any point in time.
- **Historical Revocation**: When legal custody changes or a guardian is removed, `status` transitions to `REVOKED`, populating `revokedAt`, `endDate`, and `revokedReason`.
- **Legitimate Restoration**: If a previously revoked relationship is reinstated (e.g. court order reversed, family reconciliation), `linkGuardianToStudent` reactivates the existing relationship record, sets `status = ACTIVE`, clears `revokedAt`/`revokedReason`, and writes an audit log, without violating unique constraints.

### 4.2 Primary Contact Transactional Invariants
1. **Exactly One Primary Contact**: Every student with active guardian relationships must have exactly one primary contact.
2. **Default Promotion**: The first guardian linked to a student is automatically designated as primary contact (`isPrimaryContact: true`).
3. **Atomic Demotion/Promotion**: When a new or existing guardian is promoted to primary contact:
   - Within `prisma.$transaction`, any existing active relationship for that student with `isPrimaryContact: true` is atomically demoted to `isPrimaryContact: false`.
   - The target relationship is set to `isPrimaryContact: true`.
4. **Auto-Reassignment on Revocation**: If the current primary contact relationship is revoked:
   - The engine queries the remaining active relationships for that student (ordered by `createdAt ASC`).
   - If another active relationship exists, it is automatically promoted to primary contact.
   - If no other active guardians exist, primary contact remains unassigned until a new guardian is linked.

## 5. Multi-Programme Simultaneous Enrollment Architecture

Swanford Academy uniquely operates an integrated curriculum where students may be concurrently enrolled in Western academic schooling and classical Quranic/Tahfeez programmes.

### 5.1 Programme Hierarchy & Term Boundaries
- **Main Academic Programme**: Creche, Pre-Scholars, Pre-Nursery, Nursery, and Primary (`EnrollmentType.MAIN_ACADEMIC`).
  - **Rule**: A student may have **at most one** active `MAIN_ACADEMIC` enrollment in any given academic term.
  - Attempting to enroll a student in Primary while actively enrolled in Nursery in the same term is strictly rejected.
- **Additional Programme**: Tahfeez Halaqah (`EnrollmentType.ADDITIONAL_PROGRAMME`).
  - **Rule**: A student may have one or more active `ADDITIONAL_PROGRAMME` enrollments concurrently with their main academic enrollment.

### 5.2 Academic Progression & Lineage Preservation
- When advancing a student to a new term or session (`progressStudentEnrollment`):
  1. The prior term's enrollment is marked as `COMPLETED` (`endDate = now()`).
  2. A new `ACTIVE` enrollment record is created for the target session/term/class.
  3. Historical enrollments remain permanently in the database for auditing and academic transcripts.

## 6. Field-Level Sensitive Data Visibility Matrix

Medical conditions, clinic treatment notes, and emergency contacts carry strict privacy requirements under Nigerian child data protection standards and institutional policy:

| Role / Permission | Demographic Data | Classroom Safety (Allergies, Medical Conditions, Emergency Contact) | Clinical Data (Blood Group, Genotype) | Confidential Clinic Notes (`medicalNotes`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Super Admin** | Full Access | Full Access | Full Access | Full Access |
| **Admin** (with `STUDENT_MEDICAL_VIEW`) | Full Access | Full Access | Full Access | Full Access |
| **Admin** (without medical view) | Full Access | Masked (`null`) | Masked (`null`) | Masked (`null`) |
| **Teacher** (Assigned Class Scope) | Full Access | **Visible** (Classroom safety) | **Masked (`null`)** | **Masked (`null`)** |
| **Parent** (Linked Own Child) | Full Access | **Visible** | **Visible** | **Masked (`null`)** (Confidential clinic records) |
| **Parent** (Other Children) | **403 Forbidden** | **403 Forbidden** | **403 Forbidden** | **403 Forbidden** |
| **Accountant** | **Visible** (Billing demographic) | **Masked (`null`)** | **Masked (`null`)** | **Masked (`null`)** |

### 6.1 Generic Student List Query Projections
Generic list queries (`listStudents`) invoked for administrative dashboards, registers, or billing tables **never project medical or emergency fields under any circumstances**, regardless of the caller's role. Detailed sensitive information is only accessible via explicit single-student queries (`getStudentById`).

## 7. Duplicate Student Detection & Guardian Matching Engines

### 7.1 Duplicate Student Detection (`detectPotentialStudentDuplicates`)
- **Warning Only**: Does **never** automatically merge students.
- **Criteria**: Matches on exact `firstName` + `lastName` (case-insensitive) + `dateOfBirth`.
- **Justification**: In Dutse, twins or close family names with identical birth dates occur legitimately. The system alerts administrative staff to verify identity without blocking valid registrations or corrupting data.

### 7.2 Conservative Guardian Matching (`matchExistingGuardian`)
- **Strict Matching**: Matching is performed exclusively on verified, normalized, lowercase email address.
- **Anti-Merge Guardrails**: Same-name or same-phone without matching email **never** auto-merges. Instead, the engine flags a conflict warning (`hasConflict: true`), requiring staff review to prevent linking unrelated children to the wrong parent.

## 8. Integration with Bulk Enrollment Pipeline

The Stage 6 services directly interface with the existing Stage 2D/3 `processStudentBulkImport` pipeline:
1. `validateStudentImportBatch` checks duplicate warnings and guardian match conflicts.
2. `executeStudentImportBatch` invokes Stage 6 `createStudent`, `createGuardian`, `linkGuardianToStudent`, and `enrollStudentInProgramme` within individual row transactions.
3. Outbox invitations are dispatched for newly provisioned parent portal user accounts.
